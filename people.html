Bart Ellenbroek

Group leader

Bart obtained his PhD in 1988 from Radboud University Nijmegen in the Netherlands and, after working in several countries, joined Victoria University of Wellington in 2012. Trained as a behavioural neuroscientist and pharmacologist, his research initially focused on developing animal models for psychiatric disorders such as schizophrenia, drug addiction and autism spectrum disorders.

More recently he has become fascinated by how the brain — and behaviour — change over time. This has led him to investigate neurodevelopment in animals and humans, and how drugs such as alcohol and psychedelics change our brain and behaviour. His work spans animal research (rats and planaria) and research in humans.

Acknowledging the enormous complexity of the brain, he is a strong proponent of collaboration and has established many past and ongoing collaborations. Outside the lab he is married to Jiun Youn, and has two daughters, one son, two dogs and two budgies.
