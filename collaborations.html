Collaborations

The brain is too complex to understand alone. Much of our work is done hand-in-hand with colleagues and labs elsewhere — here is who we build with.

The brain is arguably the most complex organ in our body. To understand how it functions requires expertise in many different disciplines. Our NEST group has been fortunate to have collaborated with many colleagues from all over the world.
