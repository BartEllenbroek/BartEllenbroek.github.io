Planaria Plasticity

Planaria are flatworms that have been around for about 600 million years. One of their most unique characteristics is that they can regenerate every part of their body, from as little as about 10,000 cells. This includes regeneration of an entire new nervous system within about 10 to 14 days. Although evolutionarily speaking they are very far apart from us humans, their nervous system is remarkably similar. Thus they have two hemispheres and the same neurotransmitters (such as dopamine, serotonin, GABA, glutamate etc.) as we humans have. Moreover, their pharmacology shows strong similarity with rats and humans. For this reason we are very interested in understanding planarian brains and behaviour.

Our research on planarians is quite broad and involves understanding the effects of drugs, such as psychedelics and drugs of abuse, investigating approach and avoidance behaviour, and studying cognitive processes.
